# Design system — Dreem Sabiniano portfolio

## Direction
Keep the existing warm cream, blush, and pink identity, with clearer sales messaging, project previews, and restrained motion. Use the supplied reference for concise service cards, a practical VA fit check, and a persistent booking action. No gradients or dark theme.

## Color
- Background `#fff8f3`; surfaces `#fffdfb` and `#fae6ed`.
- Blush `#f5dce5`; deeper blush `#edc5d3`.
- Pink `#c96f90`; deep pink `#ad4e73`.
- Heading ink `#452d3a`; body `#493a42`; muted copy `#66515c`.
- Border `#d9b5c1`; footer plum `#432b38`.

## Typography
- Playfair Display, 500–600, with Georgia fallback for expressive headings.
- DM Sans, 400–700, with Arial fallback for body and controls.
- Hero type scales between 3rem and 6rem; section heads between 2.4rem and 4rem.

## Spacing and layout
- Use a 4px-based scale with common steps of 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 72, and 100px.
- Content max-width 1160px. Service and project grids use three columns on desktop, two on medium screens, and one on mobile.
- Project screenshots use a shared frame and aligned card details.

## Shape and depth
- Pills: 999px; controls: 12–15px radius; cards: 16–18px; portrait frames: arched.
- Thin blush borders, soft plum shadows, and the pink offset portrait edge provide depth.

## Components and motion
- Pink pill CTA with an offset blush shadow, hover lift, and visible keyboard focus.
- Service/project cards lift and use restrained pointer tilt on desktop.
- Hero uses orbit shapes, parallax, and a slow decorative sparkle. Tools move in a marquee that pauses on hover.
- Native details/summary disclosures align Problem, Findings, and Result rows.
- Reveal animations and marquee respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Imagery
- Use the supplied portrait for the hero.
- Retain supplied calendar, document, sourcing, and template screenshots.
- The two new Notion project visuals are illustrative dashboard mockups pending the screenshots you plan to provide.
